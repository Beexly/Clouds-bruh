Create founder-designed Lumera product drafts.

Steps:
1. Start from `/cockpit` candidate cards or Garrett's product brief.
2. Use `Design Variant` to create a `lumera_product_design` draft.
3. Prefer Printify or Printful for house-label products.
4. Require sample approval for apparel, expensive items, new suppliers, or unknown quality.
5. Label AI-generated media in product metadata when used.

Expected output:
- Product design draft id.
- Preferred provider.
- Sample requirement reason.

Do not proceed if:
- Artwork rights are unclear.
- Product requires certification that cannot be verified.
- Founder has not approved sample spend.

Recovery:
- Keep the design in draft.
- Route to Artisan/Sourcer for asset or vendor proof.
